About Me

Gear meshing — where intention meets motion.

The philosophy, skills and tools behind eight years of building on the web.

gear meshing, noun — the mechanical engagement of two or more gears in which the teeth of one gear interlock with the teeth of another, allowing the transmission of motion and torque between rotating shafts.

In my work, web development is more than code — it is the art of connection. Like the meshing of finely cut gears, my ideas and my client's vision interlock, each tooth finding its perfect place in motion with the other.

When their goals turn, so do mine; when they move forward, I propel them further. There is no friction, only rhythm — a steady transfer of creativity, precision, and trust.

Every project becomes a living mechanism — one where purpose drives design, and design drives experience. Together, we build motion that lasts.

This is gear meshing — where imagination and intention engage, and progress begins to move.
